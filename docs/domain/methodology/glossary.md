# Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Workspace | SaaS subscription, security, configuration, and data-isolation boundary. A workspace may contain multiple legal entities. |
| Legal entity | A company or registered organization that owns fiscal identity, books, and statutory configuration. |
| Branch | An operating establishment, office, store, clinic, or service location owned by one legal entity. |
| Platform user | Global authentication identity. It has no business access by itself. |
| Membership | Relationship granting a platform user access to one workspace. |
| Role | Workspace-defined collection of permissions. Starter templates may be provided but remain editable. |
| Scope | Workspace, legal-entity, or branch boundary within which a role assignment applies. |
| Module entitlement | Activation of a core or optional capability for a workspace. |
| Party | Person or organization known to the business. It may acquire customer, lead, vendor, or partner roles. |
| Customer account | Commercial profile of a party within a workspace. |
| Lead | Prospect that has not yet completed the workspace-defined qualification process. |
| Opportunity | Potential sale with stage, owner, expected value, and activities. |
| Quote | Versioned commercial offer that may be created manually or from an opportunity. |
| Sale | Confirmed exchange of items or services, independent of whether payment is complete. |
| Invoice | Fiscal or commercial document representing an amount due for a sale. |
| Receivable | Outstanding amount owed by a customer, with allocations and due dates. |
| Cash session | Branch/register/user operating period used to reconcile tender movements. |
| Item | Sellable or consumable product, service, membership, or other catalog entry. |
| Stock ledger | Append-only history of inventory increases, decreases, transfers, and corrections. |
| Appointment | Scheduled service commitment involving a customer, branch, resources, and staff. |
| Employee | HR profile belonging to a legal entity; optionally linked to a platform user or customer account. |
| Commission rule | Percentage or fixed-amount rule for an employee and service/item. |
| Commission accrual | Amount earned from a completed eligible business event. |
| Financial account | Bank, cash, investment, shareholder, or other balance-bearing account. |
| Liability | Debt obligation whose payments reduce an outstanding principal. |
| Incident | Operational or HR issue with type, priority, assignees, evidence, and lifecycle. |
| Regional pack | Versioned fiscal, payroll, currency, numbering, and document rules for a jurisdiction. |
| ITBIS | Dominican Republic tax on transfers of industrialized goods and services. Modeled as a configurable tax rule. |
| RNC | Dominican taxpayer registration identifier. Stored under the legal entity's jurisdictional identity. |
| TSS/INFOTEP | Dominican payroll contributions represented by the regional payroll configuration. |
